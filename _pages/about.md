Welcome to my web page!

I am a PhD student in Statistics and Computer Science at [Bocconi University](https://www.unibocconi.it/it) in Milan, Italy.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Economics from the [Department of Economics and management](https://www.ec.unipi.it/) and a Master's Degree in Stochastics and Data Science from the [Department of Economics, Social Studies, Applied Mathematics and Statistics](https://www.esomas-en.unito.it/do/home.pl).

My current research interests are:
* Bayesian modelling;
* Computational statistics;
* Stochastic processes.
